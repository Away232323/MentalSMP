Rules that keep MentalSMP fair and enjoyable

💬 Discord Rules

  • Don't make any self promotion for YouTube channels or other Discord servers
  • Be respectful on the Discord
  • Don't spam in chat
  • If you see someone making an issue create a ticket and report it
2

⛏️ Minecraft Rules

  • Be respectful in chat
  • Piechart is not allowed
  • Don't cheat, this will result in a permanent ban
  • You are not allowed to rename Custom Tools in an anvil
  • It's not allowed to hide the Mace, you need to keep it in your inventory
  • It's not allowed to place or hide the Dragon Egg, you need to keep it in your inventory
  • It's not allowed to explode bases or steal villagers
  • It's allowed to steal from chests, anvils, ender chests etc.
